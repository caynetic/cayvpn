Encrypted backup

A backup keeps everything CayVPN needs to restore this setup. It is encrypted before it is saved, and your passphrase protects it.

Technical details

Backups include keys, routes, settings, certificates, and encrypted provider credentials. A root-only service creates the archive; the settings page never reads unencrypted credentials.
